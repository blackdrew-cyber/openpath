International scholarship resources

Start with these official scholarship providers and databases. They are reliable places to check opportunities for study abroad; each programme has its own eligibility rules and application dates.

Commonwealth Shared Scholarships · United Kingdom

For eligible Commonwealth applicants, including Ghanaian citizens, seeking selected one-year taught master's courses. The official page gives the eligibility rules and application route through participating universities.

Deadline: 8 December 2026, 16:00 GMT.

Fulbright Foreign Student Program · United States

Supports eligible graduate students, young professionals and artists from participating countries. Application and selection procedures are managed by the relevant U.S. Embassy or Fulbright Commission; Ghana-specific instructions apply.

Study level: Graduate study and research.
